Cardigan Care Guide

Keep your cardigans looking beautiful for years to come

🌿

Cotton Blend

  • • Machine wash cold (30°C max)
  • • Use gentle cycle
  • • Turn inside out before washing
  • • Air dry or low heat tumble dry
  • • Iron on medium heat if needed
🐑

Wool Blend

  • • Hand wash with wool detergent
  • • Use lukewarm water (max 30°C)
  • • Don't wring or twist
  • • Lay flat to dry away from direct sunlight
  • • Store with cedar balls to prevent moths
✨

Cashmere Blend

  • • Dry clean recommended
  • • Or hand wash with cashmere shampoo
  • • Never hang - always lay flat
  • • Use cashmere comb for pilling
  • • Store folded with lavender sachets

💡 General Care Tips

Storage

  • • Always fold, never hang cardigans
  • • Use breathable garment bags
  • • Keep in cool, dry place
  • • Avoid plastic bags for long-term storage

Maintenance

  • • Remove pilling with fabric shaver
  • • Rotate wearing to prevent over-use
  • • Air out between wears
  • • Address stains immediately
